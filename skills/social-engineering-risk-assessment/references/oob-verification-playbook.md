# Out-of-Band Verification (OOBV) Protocol Playbook

## Wire Transfer & Bank Detail Changes
1. **Never** use phone numbers or links provided in the requesting email.
2. Retrieve the verified phone number from the established vendor contract in ERP/CRM.
3. Initiate a live phone callback to the designated vendor contact person.
4. Require dual-authorization (two authorized signers) for any wire transfer over threshold amount.

## IT Helpdesk Password & MFA Reset
1. Prohibit resets based exclusively on static knowledge (Employee ID, Date of Birth).
2. Require manager video verification or direct push-notification approval to previously registered corporate device.
